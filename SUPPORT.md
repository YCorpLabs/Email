# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/YCorpLabs/Email/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/YCorpLabs/Email/issues/new/choose) with the bug report form.
  Remove personal data from logs and screenshots first.
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Official builds:** in the app, use **Support & feedback**, or email
  support@ycorplabs.com. Send feedback to feedback@ycorplabs.com.
